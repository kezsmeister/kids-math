# Question review for a non-reading five-year-old

Reviewed 2026-10-06. Scope: all 41 original generator variants at levels 1–3, their pictures, instructions, answer actions and help. Result: 16 formats removed, 25 retained or simplified. These are decisions for independent play in this app, not claims that every child of five has the same readiness.

## Basis

[Head Start's preschool mathematics framework](https://headstart.gov/school-readiness/article/math-preschool) includes small quantities, concrete operations, comparisons, shapes and simple patterns. It also includes creating patterns and identifying a repeating unit: retiring this app's complicated versions does not mean those mathematical ideas are inappropriate in supported play.

[Development Matters](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/development-matters--2/development-matters) supports number composition, frames, shapes and patterns, while emphasizing depth, individual development and adult support. The conservative limits and interface decisions below are our application of that guidance to the user's non-reading child; neither source mandates this exact question set.

## Every original question format

“Kept” means the existing question was judged suitable for this context; “Simplified” means the task or its difficulty was changed. Numbers within ten still begin within five, with gradual progression; only the two optional counting activities use 11–20.

## Integration checks

Removed formats have no generator branch or parent-practice entry. They cannot be selected by mixed rounds, stale focused practice or saved follow-ups. The optional 11–20 screen offers only Count and Two Frames. Valid historical evidence, stars, stickers and activity totals survive; retired records do not schedule practice.

Regression tests cover the catalog, saved-state migration, concrete subtraction, persistent dots, pattern completion, quantity support, missing-part limits and completing every retained skill using its actual answer controls at every level. Existing seeded generation and offline-narration checks continue over the reduced catalog.

The browser tool denied access to the local file in this environment. Current layout and physical audio output are not browser-verified. This review is a code/content assessment informed by early-years guidance, not a clinical assessment, classroom trial or guarantee of suitability for every five-year-old.
